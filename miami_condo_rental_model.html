Bespoke Underwriting Miami Beach Condo Rental Model

Rental underwriting for a single two-bedroom Miami Beach condo: purchase and closing costs, financing or all cash, rent, HOA, property tax, insurance, reserves and management. Every output recalculates as you edit an input.

Step One

InputsCondo Assumptions

Default assumptions

Miami Beach / Florida, two-bedroom condo

These defaults are starting assumptions, not sourced facts. Price $400,000 and rent $2,750 / month are the scenario inputs. HOA $800 / month, HO-6 insurance $2,600 / year, a 5% maintenance reserve, 8% vacancy and 10% management are typical-range assumptions for a Miami Beach two-bedroom. Property tax at 1.9% of price approximates a Miami-Dade non-homestead millage applied to the purchase price. Closing costs at 3% approximate Florida buyer costs, including mortgage documentary stamps (0.35%) and intangible tax (0.2%) when financed; lower it for an all-cash purchase. Replace each one with building-specific figures (HOA budget, special assessments, insurance quote, tax estimate) before relying on the results.

How it works. Year-1 figures drive the key metrics. NOI = collected rent minus management, maintenance reserve, HOA, property tax and insurance (before debt service). Cap rate = NOI ÷ purchase price. Cash-on-cash = annual pre-tax cash flow ÷ (down payment + closing costs). Break-even rent is solved directly: the monthly rent at which year-1 cash flow is zero, given vacancy and the percent-of-rent costs.

What is not included. Income taxes and depreciation, special assessments, appreciation and sale proceeds, furnished or seasonal rental pricing, short-term rental rules, and building-specific association restrictions on leasing.

For illustration and education only. Not investment, tax, legal or lending advice. Check every output against your own underwriting before relying on it.
